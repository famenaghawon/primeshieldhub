Professional Biography

Freedom Evbakoe Amenaghawon is a Nigerian entrepreneur and technology-focused business executive. He is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of PrimeShieldHub Gadgets Limited.

He established PrimeShieldHub with a vision to build a trusted technology and consumer electronics enterprise delivering authentic products, dependable commerce and customer-focused business services while maintaining a strong commitment to professionalism and long-term value creation.

His professional background combines scientific education in Microbiology with practical business experience and professional training in data analytics, programming, cloud computing and digital technologies.

Founder Journey & Company Growth

Freedom Evbakoe Amenaghawon founded PrimeShieldHub Gadgets Limited following years of entrepreneurial experience, skills development and technology education. The company has expanded into technology retail, building materials and technology-enabled business services while maintaining a commitment to customer satisfaction and sustainable growth.
