Why I Founded PrimeShieldHub

A personal reflection on entrepreneurship, resilience, technology and the vision behind PrimeShieldHub.

Published: June 2026 Author: Freedom Evbakoe Amenaghawon Role: Founder & CEO, PrimeShieldHub Gadgets Limited

People often see a company after it begins to grow, but they rarely see the experiences, challenges and lessons that shaped its foundation. PrimeShieldHub was not created overnight. It emerged from years of learning, personal experiences, setbacks, experimentation and a determination to build something meaningful.

My journey into entrepreneurship began long before the incorporation of PrimeShieldHub Gadgets Limited. Like many young Nigerians, I faced uncertainty, financial challenges and difficult decisions while trying to create a path toward a better future. Those experiences taught me valuable lessons about resilience, adaptability and the importance of creating opportunities rather than waiting for them.

In 2020, I experienced one of the most significant turning points of my life. It was the year I lost my father and also gained admission into the University of Benin. While my original ambition was to study Medicine and Surgery, circumstances led me to pursue a Bachelor of Science degree in Microbiology. Although it was different from the path I initially envisioned, it became an opportunity to grow, learn and develop a broader perspective on life and business.

During this period, I became increasingly interested in entrepreneurship. I understood that education was important, but I also believed practical skills and business experience were equally valuable. Before commencing university studies, I learned catering as a vocational skill. While in school, I explored different income-generating activities and small business opportunities that helped me understand customers, operations and financial management.

One of the most ambitious decisions I made as a student was acquiring a Toyota Corolla through a higher-purchase arrangement and deploying it for ride-hailing services through the Bolt platform. Managing such a venture while pursuing a university degree was challenging, but it provided practical lessons that no classroom could fully teach. I learned about risk, responsibility, customer service, maintenance costs, operational efficiency and the realities of running an income-generating asset.

Those experiences gradually shaped my understanding of entrepreneurship. I came to realize that sustainable businesses are built through discipline, consistency and a willingness to solve real problems. Success is rarely the result of a single breakthrough; it is often the accumulation of many small decisions made correctly over time.

After completing my university education, I continued investing in myself. I expanded my knowledge through studies in software development, data analytics, cloud computing, business technology and digital systems. Technology fascinated me because of its ability to transform industries, increase efficiency and create opportunities at scale.

The more I learned about technology, the more convinced I became that the future would belong to businesses capable of combining innovation with practical customer solutions. This belief became one of the foundations upon which PrimeShieldHub was built.

I founded PrimeShieldHub with a simple but powerful vision: to create a trusted enterprise that delivers value through technology, commerce, innovation and strategic diversification. From the beginning, the goal was not simply to sell products. The objective was to build a company capable of solving problems, serving customers and creating opportunities for long-term growth.

PrimeShieldHub initially focused on technology products, consumer electronics and digital commerce. Over time, the business expanded its activities and explored opportunities beyond traditional retail. This expansion reflected my belief that sustainable businesses should continuously evolve, adapt and pursue opportunities where they can create meaningful value.

Building Beyond Technology

Although PrimeShieldHub began with a strong focus on technology products and digital commerce, I have always believed that entrepreneurship should not be limited by industry labels. The principles that drive successful businesses"”integrity, customer focus, operational excellence and innovation"”can be applied across multiple sectors.

This mindset influenced the gradual expansion of PrimeShieldHub into other areas of economic activity. As opportunities emerged, we explored sectors that aligned with long-term value creation, sustainable growth and practical market needs.

One example is PrimeShieldHub BuildMart, which focuses on building materials and construction-related supplies. Housing and infrastructure remain fundamental needs within every growing economy, and I believe businesses that contribute to development and community growth will continue to play an important role in the future.

Another area of focus is agriculture. Agriculture remains one of the most important sectors in Africa and represents significant opportunities for economic development, food security and wealth creation. Through cassava farming operations, I have continued to learn valuable lessons about patience, long-term planning and the importance of productive assets.

While technology and agriculture may appear very different on the surface, they share an important common principle: both create value by solving real-world problems. This belief continues to guide my approach to business and investment decisions.

Creating Opportunities

One of the most rewarding aspects of entrepreneurship is the ability to create opportunities for others. Businesses do more than generate revenue; they create jobs, support suppliers, serve customers and contribute to local economic activity.

As PrimeShieldHub continues to grow, I remain committed to building an organization that creates value not only for its customers but also for employees, partners and communities. Sustainable businesses should improve lives and contribute positively to society.

Every worker, supplier, customer and partner who contributes to the PrimeShieldHub ecosystem plays an important role in our journey. Their trust and support have been instrumental in our continued growth.

Lessons I Have Learned

Entrepreneurship has taught me that success is rarely immediate. Most meaningful achievements require patience, persistence and the willingness to continue moving forward despite uncertainty.

I have learned that continuous learning is one of the most valuable investments anyone can make. The world changes quickly, and those who remain curious, adaptable and committed to growth will always be better positioned to identify opportunities.

I have also learned that failure is not the opposite of success; it is often part of the process. Some ventures succeed, others provide lessons, but every experience contributes to growth and better decision-making.

Most importantly, I have learned that integrity matters. Trust is one of the most valuable assets any business can possess, and maintaining that trust requires consistency, honesty and a genuine commitment to serving customers well.

Looking Ahead

The journey of PrimeShieldHub is still in its early stages. While we are proud of what has been achieved so far, we remain focused on the future and the opportunities that lie ahead.

My vision is to continue building PrimeShieldHub into a respected multi-sector enterprise recognized for innovation, professionalism, customer trust and sustainable growth. Whether through technology, commerce, agriculture, construction-related services or future ventures, our mission remains the same: create value, solve problems and contribute positively to society.

I believe the future belongs to those who combine knowledge with action, innovation with discipline and ambition with purpose. That belief continues to guide both my personal journey and the direction of PrimeShieldHub.

Freedom Evbakoe Amenaghawon

Founder & Chief Executive Officer
PrimeShieldHub Gadgets Limited
