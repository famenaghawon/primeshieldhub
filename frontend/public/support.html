Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions from our customers.

How do I track my order?
You can track your order by visiting the Track Order page and entering your order reference number. You'll also receive tracking updates via email and WhatsApp.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept bank transfers, credit/debit cards via PayStack, cryptocurrency (BTC, ETH, USDT), and cash on delivery for select locations. All payments are secure and encrypted.
How long does delivery take?
Delivery times vary by location. Lagos and Abuja: 24-48 hours. Other states: 2-5 business days. International shipping: 5-10 business days. You'll receive a tracking number once shipped.
Are your products authentic?
Yes! We source all products directly from authorized distributors and manufacturers. Every product is verified authentic and comes with warranty coverage. We stand behind every item we sell.
How do I get a reseller discount?
Sign up for our Reseller Program to access wholesale pricing. Resellers get up to 25% off retail prices, priority shipping, and dedicated support.
What is your return policy?
We offer a 7-day return policy for unused items in original packaging. For defective products, we provide replacement or refund. View our full Returns Policy for details.
How do I request an invoice?
You can request an official invoice by visiting our Invoice Request page. All invoices are issued by PrimeShieldHub Gadgets Limited (RC 9283954) with full tax details.
How can I contact support?
You can reach us via WhatsApp at +234 810 353 5978, email at primeshieldhub@gmail.com, or use the contact form below. We respond within 24 hours.
