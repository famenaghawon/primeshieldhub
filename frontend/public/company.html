Meet The Founder About PrimeShieldHub
Overview

Built For Trust.
Built For Growth.

PrimeShieldHub Gadgets Limited is a Nigerian technology and consumer electronics company operating in the retail, wholesale distribution and digital commerce sectors. The company provides consumer electronics, smartphones, laptops, accessories and related technology products to individual customers and resellers across Nigeria.

The company combines technology-enabled retail operations, customer-focused service delivery and modern business practices. PrimeShieldHub is structured to serve both direct consumers and reseller networks through physical and digital commerce channels.

Founder & CEO

Freedom Evbakoe Amenaghawon

Freedom Evbakoe Amenaghawon is a Nigerian entrepreneur and technology-focused business executive. Founder and Chief Executive Officer of PrimeShieldHub Gadgets Limited.

He oversees the company's strategic direction, technology initiatives, digital operations, customer experience and business development.
