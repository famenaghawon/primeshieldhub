Overview

PrimeShieldHub Gadgets Limited ("PrimeShieldHub," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of all individuals who interact with our services. This GDPR compliance page explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 and the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023.

While PrimeShieldHub is a Nigerian company headquartered in Benin City, Edo State, we serve customers globally and are committed to upholding the highest standards of data protection for all users, regardless of their location.

This policy applies to all personal data processed through:

  • Our website: primeshieldhub.com
  • Our API services: api.primeshieldhub.com
  • Our mobile applications and progressive web apps
  • Our WhatsApp business channel (+234 810 353 5978)
  • Our email communications (primeshieldhub@gmail.com)
  • In-person transactions at our physical locations

What Personal Data We Collect

We collect only the personal data that is necessary to provide you with our products and services. The categories of personal data we may collect include:

Information You Provide Directly

  • Identity Data: Full name, username, or similar identifier
  • Contact Data: Email address, phone number, delivery address, billing address
  • Financial Data: Payment card details (processed securely via Paystack â€” we never store full card numbers), bank transfer references, cryptocurrency wallet addresses
  • Profile Data: Account credentials (hashed passwords), purchase history, wishlist items, preferences
  • Communications Data: Messages sent via WhatsApp, email, or contact forms
  • Review Data: Product reviews, ratings, and feedback you submit

Information Collected Automatically

  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, screen resolution, timezone setting
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, products viewed, time spent on pages, click patterns, navigation paths
  • Device Fingerprint: A SHA-256 hash generated from canvas, WebGL, audio, and font characteristics for fraud prevention (not reversible to identify you personally)
  • Cookie Data: Session identifiers, preferences, analytics tokens (see Cookie Policy section below)

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Sensitive personal data (race, ethnicity, political opinions, religious beliefs, health data, biometric data for identification)
  • Government identification numbers (except where required by Nigerian law for business registration verification)
  • Children's data (we do not knowingly collect data from persons under 16)

How We Use Your Data

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Order Fulfillment: To process and deliver your orders, communicate order status, and handle returns
  • Account Management: To create and maintain your account, authenticate logins, and manage preferences
  • Customer Support: To respond to inquiries, resolve issues, and provide assistance via WhatsApp, email, or phone
  • Payment Processing: To process payments securely through our payment partners (Paystack, bank transfer, cryptocurrency)
  • Fraud Prevention: To detect and prevent fraudulent transactions using device fingerprinting and behavioral analysis
  • Marketing: To send promotional offers, newsletters, and product recommendations (only with your explicit consent)
  • Analytics: To analyze website usage, improve user experience, and optimize our services
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal processes

Legal Basis for Processing

Under GDPR, we rely on the following legal bases for processing your personal data:

  • Contractual Necessity (Article 6(1)(b)): Processing your orders, managing your account, and delivering products you've purchased
  • Legitimate Interests (Article 6(1)(f)): Fraud prevention, network security, service improvement, and business analytics
  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)): Marketing communications, cookie placement (non-essential), and newsletter subscriptions
  • Legal Obligation (Article 6(1)(c)): Tax records, business registration compliance, and law enforcement requests

Your Data Protection Rights

Under GDPR and the Nigeria Data Protection Act, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Right to Access

You can request a copy of all personal data we hold about you, free of charge.

Right to Rectification

You can request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data we hold about you.

Right to Erasure

You can request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten") under certain circumstances.

Right to Restrict Processing

You can request limitation of how we process your data while disputes are resolved.

Right to Data Portability

You can request your data in a structured, machine-readable format to transfer to another service.

Right to Object

You can object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing purposes.

Rights re: Automated Decisions

You have the right not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing.

Right to Withdraw Consent

You can withdraw previously given consent at any time without affecting prior lawful processing.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer at primeshieldhub@gmail.com or via WhatsApp at +234 810 353 5978. We will respond within 30 calendar days as required by GDPR.

Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected:

  • Account Data: Retained while your account is active, plus 2 years after last activity
  • Order Data: Retained for 7 years (Nigerian tax law requirement)
  • Payment Records: Retained for 7 years (financial compliance)
  • Customer Support Messages: Retained for 2 years after resolution
  • Analytics Data: Retained for 26 months (Google Analytics default)
  • Device Fingerprints: Retained for 24 hours (then regenerated)
  • OTP Codes: Automatically deleted after 5 minutes (or upon verification)
  • Session Tokens: Expire after 24 hours of inactivity

After the retention period expires, personal data is either permanently deleted or anonymized so it can no longer be associated with you.

Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.3. Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256-GCM
  • Password Security: Passwords are hashed using PBKDF2 with 100,000 iterations and unique salts â€” we never store plain-text passwords
  • JWT Authentication: Session tokens are signed with HMAC-SHA256 and include device fingerprint binding
  • CSRF Protection: All state-changing requests require valid CSRF tokens
  • OTP Verification: Two-factor authentication via email OTP for sensitive operations
  • Rate Limiting: API endpoints are rate-limited to prevent abuse (60 requests per minute per client)
  • Device Fingerprinting: Advanced canvas/WebGL/audio/font fingerprinting for fraud detection
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: Bot protection on forms and authentication endpoints
  • Security Headers: Strict CSP, HSTS, X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy
  • Regular Audits: Automated security scanning and dependency vulnerability monitoring

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately at primeshieldhub@gmail.com. We will take steps to delete such information promptly.

Complaints & Supervisory Authority

If you believe our processing of your personal data violates applicable data protection laws, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

In Nigeria, the supervisory authority is the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC):

  • Website: ndpc.gov.ng
  • Email: info@ndpc.gov.ng

For EU residents, you may contact your local Data Protection Authority in your country of residence. A full list of EU DPAs is available at the European Data Protection Board website.

We encourage you to contact us first at primeshieldhub@gmail.com so we can attempt to resolve your concern directly before escalating to a supervisory authority.
