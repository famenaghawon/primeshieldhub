Biography

Executive Biography

The Founder Behind PrimeShieldHub

Freedom Evbakoe Amenaghawon

Freedom Evbakoe Amenaghawon is a Nigerian entrepreneur, technology professional, and business strategist recognized for combining scientific education, data analytics, technology, and commerce to build practical business solutions.

He holds a Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) degree in Microbiology and has expanded his expertise through professional training in Data Analytics, Python Programming, Java Programming, Tableau, Microsoft Excel, Power BI, Cloud Computing, and MMS.

His multidisciplinary background enables him to bridge business operations, technology systems, data-driven decision-making, and customer-focused innovation.

As Founder and Chief Executive Officer of PrimeShieldHub Gadgets Limited, he leads initiatives focused on premium technology retail, digital commerce, customer trust, and scalable technology-enabled business systems.

Beyond technology, he is also actively interested in agricultural investments, entrepreneurship, digital transformation, and emerging technologies.

Our Story

Founder Story

Building PrimeShieldHub

PrimeShieldHub was created with a simple but ambitious vision: to build a trusted Nigerian technology company where customers can confidently purchase authentic gadgets, premium accessories, and innovative technology products.

Recognizing the challenges customers often face regarding authenticity, trust, after-sales support, and reliable technology sourcing, Freedom Evbakoe Amenaghawon established PrimeShieldHub to create a customer-centric experience built on transparency, professionalism, and long-term value.

Under his leadership, PrimeShieldHub continues to evolve beyond gadget sales into a broader ecosystem of technology solutions, digital commerce, and innovation.
