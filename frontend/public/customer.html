B. INCIDENT SUMMARY

The above-mentioned customer's device was received by PRIMESHIELDHUB GADGET LIMITED at its Boundary Road Branch for charging.

Upon receipt of the device, a charging identification card was issued to the customer in accordance with the company's customer-device handling procedure.

Subsequently, while the device was in the custody of the branch, a third party came to the branch and represented that he was a brother/relative of the customer and requested the release of the device.

The device was subsequently released to the said third party by the staff member on duty without presentation of the customer's charging identification card.

The third party also indicated that he was going to withdraw money to settle the applicable charging fee and subsequently left the premises with the device.

The original customer later returned to the branch with the charging identification card and reported that he had not authorized the third party to collect the device and did not know the person who collected it.

The incident was subsequently reported to the management of PRIMESHIELDHUB GADGET LIMITED for investigation and resolution.

At the time of the incident, the branch's CCTV system was not operational, and therefore no CCTV footage is available for the relevant period.

Following an internal review of the circumstances and in the interest of resolving the customer's loss, maintaining customer confidence and bringing the matter to an amicable commercial resolution, the company has agreed to provide the customer with a replacement device of equivalent make, model and specification.
