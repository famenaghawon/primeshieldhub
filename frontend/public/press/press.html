About

About PrimeShieldHub

PrimeShieldHub Gadgets Limited is a Nigerian technology and commerce company operating in consumer electronics, digital commerce, business development and innovation-driven solutions. The company is led by Founder and Chief Executive Officer Freedom Evbakoe Amenaghawon and is committed to building trusted customer relationships through professionalism, transparency and continuous improvement.

Leadership

Leadership & Ownership

PrimeShieldHub Gadgets Limited was founded by Freedom Evbakoe Amenaghawon. Freedom Evbakoe Amenaghawon is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of PrimeShieldHub Gadgets Limited. As Founder and CEO, Freedom Evbakoe Amenaghawon leads strategic planning, business development, technology initiatives, operational growth and long-term corporate direction. PrimeShieldHub Gadgets Limited operates from Benin City, Edo State, Nigeria.
