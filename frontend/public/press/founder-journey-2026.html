Freedom Evbakoe Amenaghawon Shares Entrepreneurial Journey

Founder and CEO of PrimeShieldHub Gadgets Limited discusses resilience, entrepreneurship, technology, agriculture and long-term business growth.

Published: June 2026 Author: Freedom Evbakoe Amenaghawon Source: PrimeShieldHub Gadgets Limited

Benin City, Edo State â€” June 2026

Freedom Evbakoe Amenaghawon, Founder and Chief Executive Officer of PrimeShieldHub Gadgets Limited, continues to expand a growing portfolio of businesses spanning technology retail, building materials, agriculture and digital services.

His entrepreneurial journey reflects a commitment to resilience, continuous learning and long-term value creation. From humble beginnings as a university student navigating personal loss and financial challenges, Freedom has steadily built multiple business operations across diverse sectors of the Nigerian economy.

In 2020, following the loss of his father and his admission into the University of Benin, Freedom began a new chapter that would significantly shape his future. Although his original ambition was to study Medicine and Surgery, he ultimately pursued a Bachelor of Science degree in Microbiology while simultaneously developing practical vocational and entrepreneurial skills.

During the waiting period before commencing university studies, he acquired catering skills as a practical trade. While pursuing his degree, he actively explored several business ventures and income-generating opportunities, developing first-hand experience in customer service, operations management and business development.

One of his most ambitious ventures during his undergraduate years involved acquiring a Toyota Corolla through a higher-purchase arrangement facilitated by LAPO. The vehicle was subsequently deployed for ride-hailing operations through the Bolt platform while he continued his university education.

These experiences provided practical lessons in entrepreneurship, financial responsibility, risk management and operational executionâ€”principles that would later contribute significantly to the establishment and growth of PrimeShieldHub.

Following graduation, Freedom expanded his professional capabilities through extensive training in technology-related disciplines including software development, data analytics, cloud computing and digital business systems. This investment in technical knowledge further strengthened his vision of building a modern enterprise capable of serving customers through innovation and technology-driven solutions.

The result was the continued growth of PrimeShieldHub, a business ecosystem focused on technology products, digital commerce, customer solutions and strategic expansion into complementary sectors.

Today, PrimeShieldHub's operations include multiple gadget and accessories retail outlets providing smartphones, laptops, accessories and technology products to customers across its locations. The company has continued investing in customer experience, operational systems and business infrastructure to support long-term growth.

Beyond technology retail, the PrimeShieldHub ecosystem also includes PrimeShieldHub BuildMart, a building materials division serving customers within the construction and infrastructure sectors. This expansion reflects the company's broader commitment to participating in industries that contribute to economic development and value creation.

The business portfolio further extends into agriculture through cassava farming operations covering multiple hectares of farmland. These agricultural investments form part of a long-term diversification strategy designed to create sustainable growth opportunities while contributing to food production and rural economic activity.

Additional business activities include Charge & Pay and other supporting commercial services that complement the broader PrimeShieldHub ecosystem.

Economic Impact & Employment

Through its various business operations, PrimeShieldHub continues to contribute to local economic activity by creating employment opportunities, supporting suppliers, serving customers and investing in long-term business infrastructure.

The company's growth reflects a commitment to sustainable enterprise development while maintaining high standards of professionalism, customer service and operational integrity.

As PrimeShieldHub continues to expand, the organization remains focused on building trusted relationships with customers, business partners and communities while exploring new opportunities across technology, commerce, agriculture and innovation.

Vision For The Future

Looking ahead, Freedom Amenaghawon remains focused on expanding PrimeShieldHub into a recognized multi-sector enterprise driven by innovation, customer trust and sustainable growth.

The long-term vision includes continued expansion in technology retail, digital services, agriculture, construction-related supply chains and emerging opportunities capable of creating meaningful economic value.

PrimeShieldHub's leadership believes that entrepreneurship, technology and strategic diversification will remain important drivers of growth for the company in the years ahead.
