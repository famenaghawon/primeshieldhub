A Letter From The Founder

June 10, 2026
Dear Customers, Partners, and Friends,

PrimeShieldHub was founded on a simple belief: trust should never be optional in commerce.

When I launched PrimeShieldHub Gadgets Limited, my goal was never simply to sell technology products. It was to build something different "” a company where trust is not just a marketing word but the foundation of every transaction, every relationship, and every decision.

Throughout my early experiences as a student, entrepreneur, and technology enthusiast in Benin City, I repeatedly encountered the same challenge: customers wanted confidence, businesses wanted reliability, and both often struggled to find it. I saw small business owners struggle to access quality inventory. I saw customers hesitate because they had been disappointed before. These experiences stayed with me.

PrimeShieldHub was created to solve that problem. We exist to make authenticity the standard, not the exception. We exist to serve customers who deserve better and partners who dream of building something meaningful.

This journey has not been without challenges. From navigating the uncertainty of university years to building a business from the ground up, I have learned that resilience is not optional "” it is essential. But I have also learned that when you build on a foundation of integrity, the right people find you. The right customers stay. The right partners grow with you.

I believe Africa's future will be shaped not only by technology itself, but by the trust, infrastructure, and opportunities built around it. Businesses that solve real problems with integrity will define the next generation of economic growth across the continent. PrimeShieldHub intends to be part of that transformation.

Today, PrimeShieldHub is more than a gadget company. We are an ecosystem spanning technology retail, digital commerce, building materials, and agricultural investments. But at our core, we remain what we have always been: a company that puts people first.

To every customer who has trusted us with their business: thank you. To every partner who has believed in our vision: thank you. To every team member who has contributed to this mission: thank you.

The future holds more. More innovation. More opportunity. More trust. I invite you to be part of what comes next.

Freedom Evbakoe Amenaghawon
Founder & Chief Executive Officer
