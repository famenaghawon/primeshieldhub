Items must be returned within 14 days of receipt, unused and in original packaging with all seals intact.

Warranty Coverage

All PrimeShieldHub products come with a manufacturer's warranty. Coverage varies by product category.

Smartphones & Tablets

12-month manufacturer warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship.

Audio Devices

6-month warranty on AirPods, headphones, and speakers.

Laptops & Computers

24-month warranty on MacBooks and other premium laptops.

Accessories

6-month warranty on chargers, cables, and power banks.

Warranty Exclusions: Physical damage, unauthorized repairs, water damage, and normal wear and tear are not covered. Proof of purchase is required for all claims.
