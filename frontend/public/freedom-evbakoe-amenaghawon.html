BIOGRAPHY

Professional Biography

Freedom Evbakoe Amenaghawon is a Nigerian entrepreneur and Founder & Chief Executive Officer of PrimeShieldHub Gadgets Limited. He has developed interests in technology, digital commerce, construction supplies, agribusiness and long-term enterprise development.

Through PrimeShieldHub and its business divisions, he has pursued opportunities across consumer electronics, building materials and technology-driven business solutions. His entrepreneurial approach is centered on continuous learning, innovation, customer value and sustainable growth.

His professional journey combines scientific education in Microbiology with practical business experience and professional training in data analytics, programming, cloud computing and digital technologies, resulting in a multidisciplinary perspective on entrepreneurship and value creation.

EDUCATION

Education & Academic Background

Freedom Evbakoe Amenaghawon earned a Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) degree in Microbiology from the University of Benin, Nigeria.

His scientific education strengthened his analytical thinking, research discipline and evidence-based approach to problem-solving, providing a solid foundation for his later work in technology, data analysis and business development.

Prior to university, he also developed practical vocational skills in catering, gaining early experience in service delivery, quality control and customer satisfaction.

During his university years, Freedom explored several entrepreneurial ventures, gaining practical experience in operations, customer service, financial management and business development.

PRIMESHIELDHUB

Founder of PrimeShieldHub

Freedom Evbakoe Amenaghawon is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of PrimeShieldHub Gadgets Limited, a Nigerian enterprise focused on technology retail, digital commerce and technology-enabled business solutions.

PrimeShieldHub was established with the objective of providing authentic technology products, dependable customer service and sustainable business solutions while maintaining high standards of professionalism and integrity.

Under his leadership, PrimeShieldHub has continued expanding its operations and exploring opportunities across multiple sectors of the economy.
