Are your invoices official?

Yes. All invoices are issued by PrimeShieldHub Gadgets Limited (RC 9283954), a CAC-registered Nigerian company with full tax compliance (TIN 2621297539968).

Can I use this for tax purposes?

Absolutely. Our invoices include all required company details, TIN, and transaction information suitable for tax filing and business expense documentation.

What if I need a corporate invoice?

Select "Business / Corporate" as the invoice type and provide your company name and TIN. We'll issue a corporate invoice with your business details.
