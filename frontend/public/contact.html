Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our 2026 products, services, and support channels.

How do I track my support ticket?

You can track your support ticket using the "Track Existing Ticket" section above by entering your Ticket ID (emailed to you upon submission).

What's the difference between Live Chat and WhatsApp support?

Live Chat provides instant AI responses 24/7 for general questions. WhatsApp offers direct human support during business hours for urgent matters.

How long does it take to get a response to my support ticket?

Response times: Critical (1-2 hours), High (4-6 hours), Medium (12-24 hours), Low (24-48 hours).

Do you offer phone support for all customers?

Phone support is available to all customers with active orders, registered resellers, and business clients during business hours (8am-10pm daily).

Can I visit your physical location to pick up my order?

Yes, pickup is available at our Lagos location for pre-paid orders. Contact us via WhatsApp to schedule pickup after payment confirmation.

What should I do if my issue isn't resolved by the support team?

You can escalate your issue by replying to your ticket email with "ESCALATE" in the subject or messaging us on WhatsApp with "Manager Escalation".
